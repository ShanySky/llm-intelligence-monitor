# Task: review a proposed Spring service change

Read `context.md` and `ProposedPatch.java`. Write `REVIEW.md` containing only substantive correctness/reliability findings. Prioritize issues that can cause wrong behavior in production; do not spend findings on formatting or naming.

For every finding include:
- a short title;
- why it can fail;
- the minimal robust direction for fixing it.

End with a one-line verdict: `VERDICT: REQUEST_CHANGES` or `VERDICT: APPROVE`.
